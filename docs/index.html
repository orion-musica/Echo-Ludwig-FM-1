Echo Ludwig

A listening game for blind and low-vision players on the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket synthesizer. Hear a melody, play it back, watch it grow.

Free and open source, by BULØKE. Experimental: the first builds are being tested now.

Press a key: Ludwig listens, and the light follows your hand.

How to play

  1. Ludwig says “listen” and plays a short melody, a few notes long.
  2. Two short beeps mean it is your turn. Play the melody back on the keys.
  3. Played a wrong note? Ludwig tells you “higher” or “lower”, with a rising or falling sound.
  4. Every round the melody grows by one or two notes.
  5. Play the whole melody to unlock the record. Then the drop plays: the melody as a full dance track.

What the screen shows

For anyone watching, the screen stays black with one small white message.

Records earn up to three stars. Three records in a crate open the next crate. Your rank grows with your stars, from Bedroom DJ to Main Stage Legend.

Other modes: Quick Echo (records one after another on three lives), Jam (play over a beat), Library (listen to your records) and Key Explorer (press any key or button and Ludwig names it).

Made for listening

  • Every menu is spoken by Ludwig, the game's own small speech synthesizer. You never need the screen.
  • Sound icons tell you where you are: menu ticks rise in pitch from the first item to the last, and every event has its own short sound.
  • Pitch panning: in headphones, low notes sit slightly to the left and high notes to the right.
  • Your choice of talk: full, short, or sounds only. Two voices and four speeds.
  • Repeat and help: one button repeats what Ludwig said; another explains where you are.
  • Learn the keyboard: middle C is the 8th key from the left. Key Explorer names every key and button.
  • For sighted helpers: the screen stays black with one small white message, and a light wave runs over the buttons when you press them.
Light sensitivityThe game can flash its small screen white when you press a key, never more than three times a second. Turn it off in Settings, Lights. This website does not flash.

The music

25 famous melodies in five crates, each crate in its own electronic style. All of them are classical works composed before 1929, or traditional songs, so they are free for everyone to use.

Crate 1, Chiptune

Twinkle Twinkle, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells, Brahms's Lullaby, Moonlight Sonata.

Crate 2, House

Bach's Prelude in C, Minuet in G, Vivaldi's Spring, Grieg's Morning Mood, Bach's Badinerie.

Crate 3, Trance

Pachelbel's Canon, Satie's Gymnopédie, The Blue Danube, Greensleeves, Swan Lake.

Crate 4, Electro

Korobeiniki, The Entertainer, Eine kleine Nachtmusik, Mozart's Symphony 40, Für Elise.

Crate 5, Hard Techno

Beethoven's Fifth, Toccata in D minor, In the Hall of the Mountain King, Chopin's Funeral March, Rondo alla Turca.

Play in your browser

The browser version runs the same code as the firmware, with the same voice and sounds. Sound on, and use headphones if you can.

Keys on a computer keyboard: the letters A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P are the piano keys. Left and right arrows move through menus. Enter selects. Escape goes back. The number keys 1 to 0 are the FM-1's other buttons; 0 repeats what Ludwig said.

If you use a screen reader, Ludwig and your screen reader will both talk. You may want to pause your screen reader's speech while you play.

Install on the FM-1

  1. Back up your FM-1 projects, voices and samples in the M-VAVE workbench.
  2. Connect the FM-1 to a computer by USB and open the installer in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Follow the installer. Keep the FM-1 connected until it says it is done.
  4. The FM-1 restarts and Ludwig says “Welcome to Echo Ludwig”.
Before you installEcho Ludwig keeps your progress in the storage place of project slot 15, so a project saved there is replaced. The installer includes the original firmware, so you can always go back.

Accessibility of this website

This website aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It works with a keyboard and with screen readers, has no flashing and no sound until you ask for it, and lets you change text size, colours and spacing above. Your choices are remembered on this device.

It has not yet been tested by blind users. If something does not work for you, please tell us on GitHub in the issues section of the project. Your report helps the next player.

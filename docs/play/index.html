Play Echo Ludwig

The same game, voice and sounds as on the FM-1, running in your browser. Sound on, headphones if you can.

Press Start, then play with your keyboard.

Keyboard starts at middle C
Before you playIf you use a screen reader, Ludwig and your screen reader will both talk; you may want to pause your screen reader's speech while you play. The game's small screen can flash white when you press a key, never more than three times a second; turn it off in the game's Settings, Lights.
